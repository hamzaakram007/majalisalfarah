# Majalis Alfarah — Wartime / High-Foam-Cost Product Pivot Analysis

**Business:** Majalis Alfarah (مجالس الفرح)  
**Location:** Yanbu, Saudi Arabia (showroom + custom fabrication)  
**Heritage:** Operating since ~1990 (30+ years)  
**Core lines today:** Sofas · Majlis seating · Foam supply · Curtains · Cushions · Accessories · Custom design  
**Problem:** Polyurethane foam (PU) is now very expensive because of regional conflict / logistics shock. New full-foam sofa production margins are under pressure.

---

## 1. What is actually happening to your cost base

Your business sits on **two foam roles**:

| Role | Meaning for you | War-period effect |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Raw material** | Foam inside every sofa / majlis seat | Gross margin collapses if you keep old retail prices |
| **Finished product** | Foam blocks, sheets, rolls sold to workshops & households | Unit sales may rise (repair demand) but buying cost rises faster |

### Why foam spiked (2025–2026 regional context)

1. **Petrochemical / isocyanate tightness** — MDI and TDI (PU foam chemicals) are regionally concentrated. Plant shutdowns and feedstock shocks in the Gulf tighten supply fast.
2. **Red Sea / Cape rerouting** — Imports of fabrics, hardware, chemicals, and spare foam add 10–14 days and higher freight + insurance.
3. **Energy & naphtha linkage** — Higher crude / naphtha feeds through into polyurethane pricing.
4. **Consumer behavior shift** — Households delay big furniture buys, repair what they have, or buy smaller “refresh” items.

**Bottom line:** Competing only on “new luxury foam sofas at 2024 prices” is the highest-risk path. The winning path is to **sell labor, fabric, refresh, curtains, services, and low-foam products** while treating foam as a scarce, premium, or B2B-only input.

## 2. Your current catalog mapped by foam risk

Based on the live product ecosystem (sofas, majlis, foam, curtains, cushions, accessories):

### Critical risk (heavy foam) — protect margin or pause volume push

- Full sofa sets (SAR 2,800–9,200)
- L-shape / modular / recliner sofas
- Full majlis seating packages (SAR 6,200–13,200)
- Soft back foam, firm seat cores, foam rolls, commercial foam bundles

**Action:** Raise prices, shrink foam volume per seat (hybrid builds), sell fewer SKUs at higher ticket + deposit, prioritize custom quotes over discounting.

### Medium risk (some foam, still sellable)

- Floor majlis with thinner pads
- Cushions / bolsters / poufs (smaller foam volume)
- Memory toppers / replacement kits (repair market)
- Outdoor pads (niche, price-sensitive)

**Action:** Push **replacement & refresh**, not full room rebuilds. Sell covers + inserts separately.

### Low / no foam risk — **push these hardest now**

- Curtains (wave, blackout, sheer, double-layer, motorized, hotel-grade)
- Measure & install kits / installation labor
- Wall panels, coffee tables, rugs, lighting (majlis décor without foam)
- Fabric protection, cleaning kits, arm trays, throws, side tables
- VIP majlis **design / consultation** (service revenue, zero foam)
- Reupholstery using customer’s existing frame (sell fabric + labor, minimal new foam)

## 3. What Saudi buyers buy in disruption periods

In Yanbu and KSA generally, wartime / crisis spending for home businesses looks like this:

| Buyer need | What they buy | Ticket size | Foam dependency |
|------------|---------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Keep home usable | Repair, reupholstery, cushion refill | Low–mid | Low–medium |
| Privacy / heat / AC bills | Blackout & thermal curtains | Mid | None |
| Hospitality still matters | Majlis refresh (covers, pillows, lighting) | Mid | Low |
| Delay big CAPEX | “Looks new” packages vs new sofa | Mid | Low |
| B2B continuity | Hotels/offices maintain, not rebuild | Mid–high | Selective |
| Cash-constrained | Small accessories, throws, cleaning | Low | None |
| Premium survivors | Custom VIP majlis (fewer, higher margin) | High | Controlled |

**Insight:** Demand does not disappear — it **downsizes and shifts to refresh + curtains + services**. Your 30-year trust and Yanbu presence are stronger assets than cheap foam inventory right now.

## 4. Priority products to sell now (ranked)

### Tier A — Immediate cash, low foam (start this week)

1. **Blackout / thermal / double-layer curtains**  
   - Strong Saudi demand (heat, privacy, AC savings).  
   - Labor + fabric margin; no foam.  
   - Bundle: measure + install + rails.

2. **Sofa / majlis reupholstery packages**  
   - “Replace fabric, keep frame.”  
   - Sell craftsmanship, not chemicals.  
   - Offer optional thin foam top-up only when necessary (priced as surcharge).

3. **Cushion & cover refresh kits**  
   - New covers + washable cases; reuse or partial refill.  
   - Embroidered Arabic pillows, bolster sets, throws — emotional refresh at low ticket.

4. **Sofa care accessories**  
   - Protection spray, cleaning kits, arm trays, throws, leg protectors.  
   - High turn, Instagram-friendly, WhatsApp impulse buys.

5. **VIP design consultation (paid)**  
   - Layout + fabric board + quote.  
   - Filters serious buyers; deposits before any foam is cut.

### Tier B — Margin repair on foam-heavy lines

6. **Hybrid seating builds**  
   - Wood / spring / webbing + thinner high-density foam only where needed.  
   - Market as “durable structure, efficient comfort” not “cheap.”

7. **Repair & custom-cut foam kits (premium priced)**  
   - Do **not** discount foam. Position as scarce specialty.  
   - Target workshops, upholsterers, hotels — B2B with MOQ and deposit.

8. **Compact / apartment sofas**  
   - Less foam volume than 6m L-shape floor majlis.  
   - Keep in catalog; drop aggressive promo on huge foam sectionals.

9. **Floor majlis with modular thin pads**  
   - Sell fabric + carpets + bolsters as the hero; pad as optional add-on.

### Tier C — New / adjacent SKUs that fit your workshop (90-day build)

10. **Curtain-first room packages** (curtains + lighting + rug + pillows — no new sofa)  
11. **Removable zip majlis covers** (washable, seasonal colors)  
12. **Fiber / cotton / wool blend fills** for decorative pillows (foam-alternative where comfort allows)  
13. **Wooden majlis frames / coffee tables / wall panels** (carpentry margin)  
14. **Hotel / camp / temporary seating rentals or refurb contracts** (if logistics allow)  
15. **B2B maintenance contracts** for furnished apartments, furnished rentals, small hotels in Yanbu industrial corridor

## 6. Pricing & offer redesign (practical)

### A. Split the product

Instead of one sofa price, sell:

1. Frame / structure  
2. Fabric & labor  
3. Foam comfort package (optional tiers: Standard / Firm / Premium)  

Customers who want maximum foam pay the true cost. Others still buy.

### B. Deposit-first production

- Custom sofas / majlis: **40–50% deposit** before cutting foam.  
- Curtains: deposit or full pay on measure confirmation.  
- Foam B2B: pay-before-cut, no open credit during shortage.

### C. “Refresh Room” offer (hero wartime offer)

**Package example (SAR 1,200–3,500):**  
New curtain set + 6–8 pillows/throws + cleaning/protection kit + optional lighting accent.  
Message: *مجلس جديد بدون تغيير الكنب* — “A new majlis look without replacing the sofa.”

### D. Repair clinic days

Weekly “foam & fabric clinic” in Yanbu: free diagnosis, paid repair. Converts walk-ins into reupholstery jobs.

## 8. Unit economics sketch (illustrative)

Assume foam cost up **+40–80%** vs prior baseline:

| Offer | Foam share of COGS | Resilience | Notes |
|-------|--------------------|------------|-------|
| Full 6m majlis sofa | Very high | Poor | Raise price hard or pause promo |
| Reupholstery (fabric+labor) | Low | Excellent | Best use of 30-year skill |
| Blackout curtains + install | None | Excellent | Scale labor team |
| Pillow cover refresh | Very low | Excellent | Volume product |
| Custom-cut foam kit | 100% foam | OK if premium | Sell scarcity, not volume |
| Design consultation | None | Excellent | Funnel for deposits |

**Rule of thumb:** Every hour of skilled sewing/install should out-earn every kilogram of foam you burn.

## 10. Strategic verdict

**You are not only a foam company.** You are a **Yanbu hospitality-furniture craft house**: fabric, sewing, majlis culture, curtains, and trust since 1990.

In war / high-foam-cost conditions, the products that protect the business are:

1. **Curtains + installation**  
2. **Reupholstery & seat repair**  
3. **Cushions, covers, throws, accessories**  
4. **Paid design / custom quotes with deposits**  
5. **Premium, scarce foam sold carefully to B2B — not as a loss leader**  
6. **Full new foam sofas only at true cost, for customers who still pay**

Do not wait for foam to become cheap again to make money. **Sell the skills that do not depend on polyurethane.**

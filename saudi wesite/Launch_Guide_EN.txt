
Saudi Furniture Ecosystem - Launch Instructions (English)

1. Upload all website files to your hosting account (public_html).
2. Connect your domain name through your hosting or Cloudflare.
3. Configure payment gateways (Mada, STCPay, Apple Pay).
4. Add real product content through Admin Panel.
5. Link social media accounts from the footer/header.
6. Test both Arabic and English language pages.
7. Promote launch on social media using the kit provided.

Support: Contact your technical team or hosting provider.

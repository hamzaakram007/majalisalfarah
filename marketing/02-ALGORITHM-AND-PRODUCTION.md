# Algorithm Strategy & Reel Production Standards

Sources reviewed for this playbook (platform-reported / reputable 2026 creator guides): Instagram emphasises **watch time**, **likes**, and **sends (DM shares)** — sends matter more for non-follower reach; saves matter for Explore utility content. TikTok heavily weights **watch time** and **completion** on the For You feed. Treat all of this as directional — never as a guaranteed formula.

## What we optimise for

| Signal | How we design for it |
| --- | --- |
| Watch time / retention | Hook in 0–3s; cut dead air; every scene earns its place |
| Completion | Match length to idea (often 12–25s for product proof; longer only if retention holds) |
| Sends | Make content “send to spouse / family” — decisions & reveals |
| Saves | Checklists, mistakes, comparisons, fabric guides |
| Comments | Binary choices, ratings, city replies |
| Profile visits / follows | Consistent visual identity + series naming |
| WhatsApp / site / visits | Clear end CTA; geo-honest offers |

**Rules**

1. Capture attention immediately — no logo stings longer than 0.5s at open.  
2. Payoff early; deepen after.  
3. Subtitles always.  
4. Prefer original footage of real products and installs.  
5. Test multiple hooks on the same body cut.  
6. Vary duration deliberately; read retention graphs.  
7. Recurring series train topical relevance.  
8. Never promise virality from hashtags, posting time, or a magic length.

## Flexible reel structure

| Beat | Timing guide | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 0–3s | Scroll-stop hook (visual + text) |
| Story | 3–7s | Problem, desire, or tension |
| Presentation | 7–15s | Product, craft, or transformation |
| Conversion | Final seconds | One CTA |

Adjust freely by concept. Transformation reveals may compress story; education may extend presentation.

## Full production package checklist

Every reel brief must include:

1. Campaign objective  
2. Target audience  
3. Creative concept (+ why it should work)  
4. Hook alternatives (min. 3)  
5. Complete storyboard  
6. Scene-by-scene descriptions  
7. Camera angles  
8. Camera movement  
9. Lighting  
10. Background / environment  
11. Editing & transitions  
12. Arabic voiceover  
13. English voiceover  
14. Arabic on-screen text  
15. English on-screen text  
16. Music / sound design  
17. Captions  
18. CTA  
19. Hashtags  
20. AI video prompts (or “use real footage only” recommendation)

## AI video & image standards

### Product accuracy (critical)

When real product photos or showroom video exist:

- Preserve exact design, fabric colour, texture, proportions, cushions, stitching  
- Do not invent features or swap furniture for AI “similar” pieces  
- Do not alter showroom branding/signage unrealistically  
- If the generator cannot hold product identity → use **real footage**, **subtle Ken Burns / parallax**, or conventional edit  

### Technical

- Vertical 9:16 · prefer 1080×1920+  
- Commercial lighting · smooth camera · natural people/environments  
- Colour grade without shifting true product colours  
- Readable type · no overlapping captions · no distorted furniture  
- Assume AI text/audio may need post fix — plan CapCut/Premiere finish  

### Prompt engineering posture

Prompts must be production-ready: format, lens, movement, lighting, materials, mood, negative constraints (“no morphing furniture, no text artifacts, no watermark”). Separate prompts per shot when consistency is fragile.

## Editing principles

- Hard cuts over fancy transitions unless a wipe serves the reveal  
- Pattern interrupt every 2–4s if retention dips  
- End card: brand mark small + single CTA large  
- AR and EN text versions as separate exports  
- Export with burned-in subtitles **and** keep a clean master for ads  

---

## Analytics loop

After publish:

1. Read 3-second and mid-point retention — rewrite hooks if drop is early.  
2. Compare sends/reach and saves/reach — classify content as “shareable” vs “reference”.  
3. Trace WhatsApp: ask “منين عرفتنا؟” or use unique links / UTMs where available.  
4. Promote winners with paid; retire losers; rebuild winners with new hooks only.

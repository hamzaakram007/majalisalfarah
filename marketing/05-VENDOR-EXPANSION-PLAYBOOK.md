# Nationwide Vendor Expansion Playbook

**Principle:** Curtains and cushions already supply **across Saudi Arabia**. Vendor expansion is mainly for **sofa upholstery, custom fabrication, and on-site installation** (and any heavy furniture logistics that are not yet national).  
Until a city is **Go-Live** for those services, treat related inbound interest as **demand intelligence**, not a confirmed install promise.

Priority expansion watchlist: Jeddah · Madinah · Makkah · Riyadh · other cities by enquiry volume.

---

## 1. Identify potential local vendors

Search for:

- Furniture suppliers and showrooms  
- Sofa upholstery workshops  
- Curtain ateliers / installers  
- Foam suppliers  
- Installation crews with residential experience  

Channels: Google Maps, local Instagram, trade WhatsApp groups, referrals from existing Yanbu craftsmen, B2B outreach reels (“نبحث عن شركاء”).

Capture in a simple CRM/sheet:

`City | Vendor name | Services | Instagram/Phone | Source | Status | Notes`

---

## 2. Verify quality & reliability

Minimum verification before listing as partner:

| Check | Pass criteria |
| --- | --- |
| Portfolio | Real project photos/video, not only stock |
| Site visit or video audit | Clean workshop, material storage, safety |
| Sample work | Agreed fabric/foam sample or small paid pilot |
| References | 2 recent customer contacts if available |
| Response SLA | Replies within agreed hours |
| Legal basics | Valid commercial registration / tax as required |
| Insurance / damage policy | Written handling of install damage |

Score 1–5 on quality, communication, capacity, price fairness. Advance only vendors scoring consistently high on quality + communication.

---

## 3. Negotiate supply & installation

Define per city:

- Who owns customer relationship (Majalis Alfarah brand front)  
- Who manufactures vs installs  
- Lead times by product type  
- Who handles after-sales defects  

Prefer **brand-controlled quoting** with local execution under SOPs.

---

## 4. Standardise product specifications

Create a shared spec sheet:

- Foam density ranges by product tier  
- Approved fabric categories  
- Stitching / piping standards  
- Curtain heading types, lining rules  
- Photo standards for QC (4 angles + detail)  

Partners may not freestyle specs that break brand promise.

---

## 5. Pricing & commission structures

Models to evaluate (choose one primary per city):

1. **Commission on referred jobs** — brand generates lead, vendor executes  
2. **Wholesale supply + local install fee**  
3. **Fixed service fee to brand** for quoting/brand use  

Document: list price bands, partner cost, brand margin, payment terms, cancellation rules.  
Never publish partner-specific discounts publicly without control.

---

## 6. Manage customer enquiries

Flow:

```
Lead in (IG/TikTok/WA/Web)
    → Capture city + need
    → If city = Go-Live → quote path + assign vendor
    → If city = Pipeline → demand log + polite waitlist message
    → If city = Yanbu core → direct showroom / core team
```

Unique WA labels or form fields: `city`, `category`, `urgency`.

---

## 7. Confirm service availability

Public language:

- ✅ “ستائر ومخدات — شحن لجميع مدن المملكة”  
- ✅ “تنجيد وتركيب ميداني — ينبع والمناطق القريبة”  
- ✅ “نسجّل اهتمامك للتنجيد/التركيب في [المدينة]”  
- ❌ “تنجيد وتركيب لكل مدن المملكة” until that city is Go-Live

Internal flag: `AVAILABLE | WAITLIST | UNAVAILABLE`.

---

## 8. Coordinate delivery & installation

SOP checklist:

- Measure appointment  
- Fabric confirmation  
- Production window  
- Delivery slot  
- Install photos for QC  
- Customer sign-off  

Brand receives photo proof before marking order complete.

---

## 9. Quality control

- Random audit of finished jobs (photo or visit)  
- Customer CSAT 1–5 via WhatsApp after 48h  
- Defect rate threshold → probation / removal  
- Shared blacklist of failed materials  

---

## 10. Collect feedback & feed marketing

Permissioned:

- Before/after for Reels (Pillar B)  
- Short testimonial audio  
- City-specific case studies once Go-Live  

This content fuels national awareness **without** overselling coverage.

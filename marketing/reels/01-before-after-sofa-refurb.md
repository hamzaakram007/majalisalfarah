# Reel 01 — Before & After Sofa Refurbishment

**Series:** «قبل وبعد» / Before & After Friday  
**Status:** Ready to shoot with real project assets  
**Format:** 9:16 · 20–28 seconds · separate AR and EN exports  

---

## 1. Campaign objective

Engagement + qualified WhatsApp leads for sofa upholstery / refurbishment in Yanbu and nearby areas. Secondary: sends and saves for algorithmic distribution.

## 2. Target audience

- Persona: Homeowners and families with tired majlis sofas who want renewal without full replacement cost anxiety  
- Geo: Yanbu (+ nearby) for CTA; national for organic reach  
- Language primary: Arabic (English cut after)

## 3. Creative concept

**Name:** Same Frame, New Life  

**Idea:** Open on a worn sofa that feels embarrassing for hosting, smash-cut to the same sofa after professional reupholstery — identical framing so the brain feels the contrast. End on tactile fabric + WhatsApp CTA.

**Why it should work:** Contrast + completion satisfaction drives rewatches; “send to spouse” decision utility drives Instagram sends; proof builds purchase confidence without fake discounts.

## 4. Hook alternatives

**Arabic**

1. نفس الكنب… بس المجلس صار ثاني.  
2. شفت الفرق؟ هذا تنجيد مش شراء جديد.  
3. الضيوف جايين والكنب قديم؟ شوف الحل.

**English**

1. Same sofa. Completely different majlis.  
2. Not a new sofa — a professional second life.  
3. Guests this weekend? Watch this refresh.

Test hook 1 first (curiosity + identity). Keep hooks 2–3 for paid/organic remounts.

## 6. Scene-by-scene descriptions

1. **Before hero:** Natural living room, slightly messy cushions OK — authenticity over staging.  
2. **Pain details:** Hands press sagging seat; close-up of worn piping.  
3. **Craft bridge:** 3–4 short cuts (max 0.6s each) of real workshop only.  
4. **After hero:** Identical lens height and distance; styled but believable.  
5. **Tactile proof:** Macro of new fabric weave and neat stitch line.  
6. **CTA card:** Brand wordmark small; WhatsApp line large; Yanbu service honesty.

## 7. Camera angles

- Eye-level wide (before/after match)  
- 45° three-quarter on sofa  
- Macro 50–100mm equivalent on fabric  
- Over-shoulder craft inserts  

## 8. Camera movement

- Before: static or micro-drift only  
- Reveal: gentle push-in (no whip-pan gimmicks)  
- Macro: slow lateral slider or handheld stabilized glide  
- Avoid rolling shutter distortion on sofa legs  

## 9. Lighting

- Soft daylight key from window side; bounce fill  
- After shot: slightly cleaner contrast, same colour temperature family so fabric colours stay true  
- No neon gels; no purple cyber looks  

## 10. Background / environment

Real Saudi majlis / living room. Remove branded competitor items. Keep décor consistent before→after except textiles/sofa presentation. Showroom OK if labelled honestly.

## 11. Editing & transitions

- Hard cut before→details  
- Match cut before→after (most important cut)  
- Jump cuts on craft (0.4–0.7s)  
- No glitter transitions, no fake AI morph of the sofa body  
- Subtitles lower-third safe zone; never covering the sofa seat

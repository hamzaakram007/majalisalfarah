## 6–11. Production notes

- **Match-cut critical:** tape camera height/distance for before and after  
- Angles: eye-level wide, 45° three-quarter, macro fabric, craft inserts  
- Movement: static before; gentle push-in after  
- Lighting: same colour temperature family so fabric truth holds  
- Environment: real home or showroom; get permission  
- Edit: hard details → match-cut reveal; **no AI morph of the sofa body**

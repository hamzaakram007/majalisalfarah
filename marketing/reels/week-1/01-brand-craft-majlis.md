# Week 1 · Day 1 — Brand Open: Craft + Majlis Beauty

**Series:** «مجلس الأحلام» / From Yanbu Craft  
**Status:** Ready to shoot  
**Format:** 9:16 · 15–20s · AR primary · EN cut for winners  
**CTA:** SAVE (soft brand — no hard sell)

---

## 1. Campaign objective

Brand awareness and premium perception across Saudi Arabia + Yanbu. Drive profile visits, saves, and follows. Introduce Majalis Alfarah as craft-led, not a random furniture page.

## 2. Target audience

- Persona: Homeowners and design-curious Saudis who aspire to elegant majlis spaces  
- Geo: KSA awareness; Yanbu local overlay welcome  
- Language primary: Arabic  

## 3. Creative concept

**Name:** From the Hands to the Room  

**Idea:** Open on a beautiful finished majlis detail, flash back through craft (stitch, fabric, foam touch), return to the calm finished room. Brand line lands once. No price, no pressure.

**Why it should work:** Aspiration + craft proof in under 20s; watch time from visual satisfaction; saves as “inspiration”; trains the algorithm on furniture/interior niche.

## 4. Hook alternatives

**Arabic**

1. من ينبع… حرفية تغيّر شكل بيتك.  
2. المجلس الفاخر ما يجي صدفة.  
3. شوف التفاصيل… بعدين شوف الغرفة.

**English**

1. From Yanbu — craft that changes how a home feels.  
2. A beautiful majlis isn’t accidental.  
3. Start with the detail. End with the room.

## 6. Scene-by-scene descriptions

1. Hero comfort frame — real showroom or real install, styled but lived-in.  
2. Three craft macros, each under 1.2s, real workshop only.  
3. Return to room — same colour story as opening fabric.  
4. Clean end card — no clutter, no dual language on one export.

## 7. Camera angles

- Low three-quarter on sofa  
- Extreme macro stitch / weave  
- Medium wide room plate  
- Optional ceiling-adjacent curtain edge for light play  

## 8. Camera movement

- Slow push-in and micro-orbit only  
- No whip pans; no handheld shake on hero shots  

## 9. Lighting

- Soft window key + bounce fill  
- Warm-neutral grade; do not shift true fabric colours  

## 10. Background / environment

Yanbu showroom or permissioned home. Hide competitor brands. Keep floors/walls tidy.

## 11. Editing & transitions

- Soft dissolves between craft macros (0.2–0.3s) or hard cuts if sharper feel tests better  
- Match colour from macro fabric to room hero  
- Subtitles in safe lower third; never cover the sofa seat centre

## 20. AI video-generation prompts

**Prefer real showroom/install footage.** Use AI only for abstract atmosphere if a plate is missing — never invent a branded sofa SKU.

### Optional mood B-roll (non-SKU)

```
Vertical 9:16 cinematic interior, soft daylight through sheer curtains onto a neutral Saudi majlis floor, dust motes, empty of specific branded furniture logos, photoreal, slow lateral camera move, 3 seconds, no text, no watermark.
```

### Image-to-video on real showroom still

```
Vertical 9:16. Slow push-in using the exact reference photo. Preserve every furniture design, fabric colour, cushion, and room layout. No morphing, no recolour, no added objects, no text. Soft daylight, photoreal, 4 seconds.
```

### Negative

`no morphing furniture, no wrong colours, no neon purple light, no watermarks, no AI subtitles`

# Week 1 · Day 3 — Three Curtain Mistakes

**Series:** «٣ أخطاء»  
**Status:** Ready to shoot  
**Format:** 9:16 · 25–35s · AR primary · EN cut  
**CTA:** SAVE + soft shop (curtains ship **KSA-wide**)

---

## 1. Campaign objective

Saves, shares, and authority. Secondary: traffic to curtain collection on https://majalisalfarah.shop for KSA buyers.

## 2. Target audience

- Persona: Homeowners choosing or replacing curtains; couples furnishing new homes  
- Geo: All Saudi Arabia (education + shippable product)  
- Language primary: Arabic  

## 3. Creative concept

**Name:** Three Mistakes That Quietly Ruin Curtains  

**Idea:** Numbered list reel. Each mistake shown as a quick “wrong” visual, then a one-second “better” correction using real curtain samples / room plates. End with save CTA + shop line for ready curtains.

**Why it should work:** High saveability (Instagram Explore utility); mute-friendly numbers; sendable to spouse during renovation decisions; commercial bridge without hard pressure.

## 4. Hook alternatives

**Arabic**

1. ثلاث أخطاء تخرب شكل الستارة.  
2. ستايرك قصيرة؟ هذا أول غلط.  
3. لا تطلب ستائر قبل هالثلاث نصائح.

**English**

1. Three mistakes that quietly ruin curtains.  
2. Short curtains? That’s mistake one.  
3. Don’t order curtains before these three checks.

## 5. Complete storyboard

| Time | Visual | Text / VO | Audio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Hands holding wrong-length sample against wall | Hook + `٣ أخطاء` | Soft tick / beat |
| 3–10s | Mistake 1: curtains too short / floating mid-wall | `١) الطول الغلط` → flash correct floor/kiss length | Beat |
| 10–17s | Mistake 2: ignoring lining / light bleed | `٢) بدون بطانة مناسبة` → lined vs unlined | Beat |
| 17–24s | Mistake 3: rod too narrow / skimpy width | `٣) العرض ناقص` → fuller gather | Beat |
| 24–30s | Beautiful correct curtain plate | الستارة الصح تغيّر الصالة | Music warm |
| 30–35s | CTA | احفظ · تسوق الستائر (شحن للمملكة) | Out |

## 6. Scene-by-scene descriptions

1. Hook with bold number badge.  
2. Mistake 1 demo — use tape on wall to show short vs proper drop.  
3. Mistake 2 — same fabric, lined vs unlined against window light.  
4. Mistake 3 — narrow panel vs fuller width on same rod.  
5. Hero “right” curtain in situ (showroom or install).  
6. End card with save + shop.

## 7. Camera angles

- Eye-level wall/window  
- Side profile of curtain drop to floor  
- Backlit close-up for lining lesson  
- Wide room plate for payoff  

## 8. Camera movement

- Static for mistake demos (clarity)  
- Slow tilt down for length lesson  
- Gentle push for final beauty plate  

## 9. Lighting

- Natural window key for lining comparison (critical)  
- Avoid mixed colour temperatures that fake fabric colour  

## 10. Background / environment

Showroom curtain wall or real room. Keep wall colour neutral. Real Majalis Alfarah curtain products when shown as “shop” payoff.

## 11. Editing & transitions

- Hard cuts between mistakes  
- On-screen `١ ٢ ٣` large, high contrast, RTL-safe placement (top third)  
- Pattern interrupt every ~3–4s  
- No overlapping captions

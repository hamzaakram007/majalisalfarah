# Week 1 · Day 4 — This or That Sofas

**Series:** «هذا ولا هذا؟»  
**Status:** Ready to shoot  
**Format:** 9:16 · 12–18s · AR primary · EN cut  
**CTA:** COMMENT (١ أو ٢) — pin a comment asking for votes

---

## 1. Campaign objective

Comments, sends, and follower growth. Soft brand exposure; learn audience style preference for future product creatives.

## 2. Target audience

- Persona: Broad home décor browsers, newly married, renovation browsers  
- Geo: KSA  
- Language primary: Arabic  

## 3. Creative concept

**Name:** مجلس ١ ولا ٢؟  

**Idea:** Split-screen or rapid A/B of two real sofa setups that feel meaningfully different (e.g. classic majlis floor seating energy vs modern modular; or cognac leather-look vs light bouclé — **use real showroom pieces**). Big numbers. Ask them to comment.

**Why it should work:** Binary choice maximises comments (ranking signal); fast completion; identity expression (“أنا ١”); low production complexity.

## 4. Hook alternatives

**Arabic**

1. أي مجلس تختار: ١ ولا ٢؟  
2. هذا ولا هذا؟ علق برقم.  
3. لو اليوم تجدّد مجلسك… ١ أو ٢؟

**English**

1. Which majlis would you choose — 1 or 2?  
2. This or that? Comment the number.  
3. Refreshing your living room today — 1 or 2?

## 6. Scene-by-scene descriptions

1. Title card with brand-safe background.  
2. Sofa 1 — clean hero, identical camera language to Sofa 2.  
3. Sofa 2 — same lens height, distance, lighting style.  
4. Split finale + comment CTA.  
5. Tiny brand end — no WhatsApp hard sell on this asset.

## 7. Camera angles

- Same three-quarter hero for both  
- Optional detail insert (arm / cushion) 0.5s each — only if it doesn’t break pacing  

## 8. Camera movement

- Matched gentle push on both so comparison feels fair  
- Or both fully static for maximum clarity  

## 9. Lighting

- Identical setup for A and B so colour bias doesn’t “rig” the vote  

## 10. Background / environment

Showroom. Clear visual difference between options. No clutter behind sofas.

## 11. Editing & transitions

- Hard cut or vertical wipe between 1 and 2  
- Numbers large, high contrast  
- Pin comment after publish: `١ ولا ٢؟ اكتب رقمك`

## Recurring series (brand IP)

Name series clearly so audiences return and algorithms learn topical relevance.

### 1) «قبل وبعد» / Before & After Friday

- Sofa refurb, fabric swap, curtain refresh, full majlis reset  
- Always real projects; never AI-invented transformations of real SKUs  
- Why it works: contrast + completion satisfaction + sendable proof  

### 2) «٣ أخطاء» / 3 Mistakes

- Curtain selection, fabric choice, lighting vs textiles, sofa scale  
- On-screen numbered list; mute-friendly  
- Why it works: saveability (Instagram Explore) + authority  

### 3) «تفاصيل تصنع الفرق» / Detail That Matters

- Stitching, foam density feel, lining, curtain heading, piping  
- Macro shots + honest benefit language  
- Why it works: quality perception without hard sell  

### 4) «هذا ولا هذا؟» / This or That

- Two sofa styles, two curtain moods, two colourways  
- Pin a comment asking for votes  
- Why it works: comments as ranking signal + preference data  

### 5) «من المعرض» / From the Showroom

- Walkthroughs, new arrivals, staff craft moments  
- Geo-tag Yanbu; invite visits  
- Why it works: local trust + consideration for nearby buyers  

### 6) «مجلس الأحلام» / Dream Majlis (lifestyle)

- Cinematic interiors — use real installs when possible; label inspiration clearly if mood-only  
- Separate from product ads so claims stay clean

## Concept bank (starter — original, brand-fit)

### High commercial potential

1. **Old sofa shame → proud majlis** — soft comedy open, elegant close  
2. **Curtains that “raise the ceiling”** — before/after vertical pull  
3. **Foam test** — hand press comparison (real materials only)  
4. **Fabric under natural light** — morning vs evening colour shift education  
5. **Custom size anxiety solved** — measuring → quote → finished piece  

### High reach potential

6. **Guests arriving in 48 hours — majlis panic** (relatable, then solution)  
7. **Rate this living room 1–10**  
8. **Saudi home: sofa too big for the wall** (scale education)  
9. **Which side of the room gets the curtains first?**  
10. **Silent ASMR craft** — sewing/upholstery for rewatches  

### Trust / expansion

11. **Since 1990 — one craft principle** (story, not timeline dump)  
12. **How we protect your fabric during install**  
13. **What we ask before we quote** (transparency builds trust)  
14. **Partner cities coming** — interest form via WhatsApp (demand gen only)  

Challenge weak ideas: if a concept only shows a pretty sofa with “للطلب تواصلوا”, kill it or rebuild with a hook, proof, and a single clear desire.

## Language packs (reusable)

- شفت الفرق؟ هذا نفس الكنب… بعد التنجيد.  
- ستايرك ممكن تغيّر شكل الصالة كامل.  
- لا تختار قماش الكنب قبل هالنصيحة.  
- ثلاث أخطاء تخرب شكل المجلس.

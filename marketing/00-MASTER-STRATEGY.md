# Master Marketing Strategy — Majalis Alfarah

## 1. Agency posture

We operate as an integrated creative + performance agency for a serious furniture and interior furnishing business — not as a generic content mill.

Every asset must do at least one of:

1. Stop the scroll  
2. Build trust through craftsmanship  
3. Move the viewer toward WhatsApp, website, or the Yanbu showroom  

Views without commercial signal are vanity. We track **attention metrics** and **business metrics** separately, then connect them.

---

## 2. Brand positioning

**One-line positioning (EN):**  
Majalis Alfarah is a premium, trustworthy sofa, curtains and home décor house — rooted in Yanbu craftsmanship since 1990 — creating elegant, comfortable living spaces with care for detail.

**One-line positioning (AR):**  
مجالس الفرح بيت فاخر وموثوق للكنب والستائر وديكور المنزل — حرفية ينبع منذ ١٩٩٠ — نصنع مساحات معيشة أنيقة ومريحة بتفاصيل تُحسَب.

**Emotional territory:** elegance · comfort · quality · craftsmanship · sophistication · reliability · beautiful living · attention to detail  

**Creative benchmark:** premium interior design commercials and luxury furniture photography — original, never imitative of a competitor’s identity.

**Voice**

| Arabic | English |
| --- | --- |
| Saudi conversational, warm, persuasive | Premium, clear, sophisticated |
| Natural dialect feel without slang overload | No hype, no fake urgency |
| RTL-safe typography in all finals | Separate AR/EN cuts when text is dense |

## 3. Commercial funnel

```
AWARENESS          CONSIDERATION           CONVERSION              RETENTION
Lifestyle reels →  Education + craft →     Product + WhatsApp →    Stories + reviews
National reach     Before/after, trust     Yanbu sales + leads     Referral content
```

| Stage | Content job | Primary KPI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Aspiration, beauty, brand recall | Reach, sends, profile visits |
| Consideration | Proof, education, craft | Saves, watch time, comments |
| Conversion | Specific products/services + CTA | WhatsApp starts, qualified leads, visits, sales |
| Retention | Delivery, install, aftercare | Repeat purchase, referrals, reviews |

**Hard rule:** Sales creatives only claim what can be fulfilled. Awareness creatives may speak to all of Saudi Arabia; conversion creatives prioritise Yanbu and nearby serviceable areas until vendor capacity exists elsewhere.

## 4. Audience geo-layers

### Layer A — Yanbu sales (priority for paid conversion)

- Homeowners renovating majlis / living rooms  
- Newly married couples furnishing first homes  
- Families choosing curtains and upholstery refresh  
- Landlords refreshing rental interiors  
- Local interior enthusiasts who can visit the showroom  

**CTA default:** WhatsApp quote · showroom visit · website product view  

### Layer B — Nearby cities (demand + selective fulfilment)

Treat as interest + capacity check. Confirm logistics before promising install dates.

### Layer C — National awareness (Jeddah, Madinah, Makkah, Riyadh + KSA)

Build brand recognition and inbound demand. Route enquiries into a **demand register** until a verified local vendor exists. Never sell “nationwide delivery confirmed” prematurely.

### Layer D — B2B

Interior designers, furniture partners, upholstery workshops — feed the vendor expansion pipeline (see `05-VENDOR-EXPANSION-PLAYBOOK.md`).

## 5. Primary objectives & KPI stack

### Business outcomes (north star)

| KPI | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Qualified WhatsApp enquiries | Clear product/service interest + location + intent |
| Showroom visits | Appointments / walk-ins attributed to content/ads |
| Revenue | Attributed sales from organic + paid |
| Cost per qualified lead | Ad spend ÷ qualified WhatsApp leads |
| Cost per purchase / ROAS | Only when tracking is reliable |

### Attention outcomes (supporting)

Views · reach · avg watch time · 3s retention · completion · replays · sends/shares · saves · meaningful comments · follows · website clicks  

**Do not** invent performance forecasts or guarantee virality from hashtags, posting times, or length.

## 6. Language & creative system

- Produce **Arabic-first** for Saudi social reach; English for premium bilingual brand presence and expat/affluent bilingual audiences.  
- Prefer **two language versions** of the same reel over bilingual on-screen clutter.  
- Always include: hook · voiceover (optional) · on-screen text · caption · CTA · hashtags — in both languages when the asset is bilingual by plan.  
- Subtitles for silent viewing on both platforms.

## 7. Website & conversion findings (audit note)

**Live check (this engagement):** `https://majalisalfarah.com` and `https://www.majalisalfarah.com` returned **HTTP 500**. Treat site recovery as a conversion blocker before heavy paid traffic to the domain.

### Confirmed brand data from the product codebase

- Showroom address and WhatsApp are configured.  
- Storefront supports EN/AR, custom sofa quotes, WhatsApp CTAs, gallery, blog.  
- Delivery policy in code correctly hedges: Yanbu showroom + delivery where partners exist.  
- Some marketing strings still imply broad KSA shipping — **align all ads and website headlines with real fulfilment** so trust is not broken.

### Recommended conversion improvements (no invented policies)

1. Restore site uptime; verify mobile load and Arabic RTL.  
2. Put WhatsApp above the fold on mobile with pre-filled message templates by product category.  
3. Add clear service-area messaging: “معرض ينبع — التوصيل والتركيب حسب التوفر في مدينتك”.  
4. Trust strip: since 1990 · showroom photos · craft process · real project photos (not stock).  
5. Product pages: real photography, fabric options, “طلب عرض سعر” primary CTA.  
6. Track WhatsApp clicks, quote forms, and call events (with consent).  
7. Fast path: Reel → profile → WhatsApp link in bio / Stories highlight “تواصل”.

## 8. Operating rhythm

| Cadence | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly | 3–4 short-form videos + daily Stories support |
| Weekly | Creative review: retention graphs + top sends/saves |
| Bi-weekly | Hook tests on winning concepts (same body, new open) |
| Monthly | Pillar mix audit; pause weak formats; scale winners into ads |
| Quarterly | Vendor city readiness review vs enquiry demand map |

Suggested mix per week:

1. Educational reel  
2. Transformation or product reel  
3. Lifestyle / storytelling reel  
4. Showroom, customer-proof, or engagement reel  

Adapt frequency to real filming capacity. Quality beats empty posting.

---

## 9. Ethical psychology (allowed levers)

Use: aspiration · emotional home pride · social proof (real only) · curiosity · contrast (before/after) · visual satisfaction · problem-solving · quality cues · trust · convenience · genuine scarcity  

Never: fabricated testimonials, fake discounts, invented scarcity, or pressure that contradicts availability.

---

## 10. Immediate 14-day launch sequence

1. Fix website 500 / confirm WhatsApp link in bio.  
2. Film 4 core assets: before/after sofa, curtain install, fabric/foam craft, showroom walk.  
3. Publish AR versions first; EN versions of winners.  
4. Soft boost best engagement reel (awareness/engagement objective).  
5. Launch WhatsApp lead ads only for Yanbu (+ nearby) geography.  
6. Start demand log for non-Yanbu cities (city, product, phone, date).  
7. Begin vendor outreach for the highest-demand city from that log.

## Success definition

Majalis Alfarah becomes a recognisable premium furnishing brand in Saudi Arabia — people watch, save, and send the content; Yanbu customers enquire and buy; expansion cities convert only when fulfilment is real.
